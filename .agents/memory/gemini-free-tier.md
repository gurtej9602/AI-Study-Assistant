---
name: Gemini Free-tier constraint
description: The user's provider, billing-tier, and data-use requirements for AI in the Study Assistant.
---

Use Google's Gemini API Free tier for the Study Assistant. Do not silently switch to Replit-billed AI or a paid Gemini project.

The user accepted sending study topics and student answers to Google under its Free-tier terms, where Google may use prompts and responses to improve its products.

**Why:** The user expressly requested Gemini's Free tier and accepted the associated data-use notice before connecting it.

**How to apply:** Preserve the Free-tier model and visible data-use notice. Confirm with the user before changing providers, billing tiers, or data handling.
